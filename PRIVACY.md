# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how Hush Studio ("the app"), developed by orailnoor, handles your information. The short version: the app works on your phone, has no accounts, and does not collect personal data.

## Information stays on your device

Everything you create in Hush Studio is processed and stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- scripts and project titles
- voice samples you record or import, and their transcripts
- cloned voices
- generated audio

This data is never uploaded by the app, is excluded from Android backups, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. It leaves your phone only if you export or share it yourself.

## Microphone

The app uses the microphone only while you record a voice sample, after you start the recording. Recordings are processed on your device to create a voice reference and a transcript. They are not sent anywhere.

## Network use

The app connects to the internet in two situations only:

1. **Speech model download.** When you choose to download the speech model, the app downloads it from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). No information about you or your content is sent. Hugging Face, like any website, may log standard connection details such as your IP address; see the [Hugging Face privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy).
2. **Reports you choose to send.** If you use **Report this audio**, the app sends the following to a database operated by the developer on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore):
   - the reason you selected and any note you wrote
   - the text of the narration you reported
   - whether a built-in or cloned voice was used
   - the app version, the Android version, and the time of the report

   No audio, voice recordings, contact details, account identifiers or advertising identifiers are sent. Reports are used only to review content that may break the app's rules and to improve the app. Reports are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Google processes this data on the developer's behalf; see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you are offline, the report is kept on your phone and sent the next time you are connected.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics, tracking or advertising
- No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties
- No access to your contacts, location, photos or other files, except audio files you choose to import

## Exported audio

Audio you export is labelled inside the file as AI-generated speech made with Hush Studio. You are responsible for how you use and share exported audio. Only clone voices you have permission to use.

## Children

Hush Studio is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

You can delete any voice or project in the app at any time, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data. To ask about a report you sent, or to request its deletion, contact the developer through the issue tracker at https://github.com/orailnoor/hush-studio/issues.
